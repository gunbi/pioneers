# Map the customer journey

[Chinese edition](SKILL.md)

1. Choose the user, task and journey scope. Define the starting point and the outcome that marks completion. Specify the people and stages represented in the source material.
2. Arrange the stages in the order users encounter them. For each stage, record the user's goal, actions, touchpoints, observed obstacles and evidence source. Label gaps as assumptions or unknowns and record how to verify them. Describe emotions and frequency only as far as the material supports.
3. Identify the breakdowns with the greatest impact on task completion. Prioritize places where users get stuck, cannot recover or must repeat work. Use the available evidence to explain priority and uncertainty.
4. Hand off a concise journey table with stages, touchpoints, outcomes, evidence or assumptions, obstacles and opportunities for validation. Pass the breakdowns worth addressing to the product experience design task to refine the page flow and acceptance criteria.
